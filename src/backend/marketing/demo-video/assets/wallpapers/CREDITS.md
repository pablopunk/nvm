# Wallpaper credits

Every photo here is CC0 (public domain dedication). They are Unsplash uploads that Wikimedia Commons imported under
CC0. Each one is cropped to 1920 × 1080 and re-encoded as WebP, with its metadata removed. The theme wallpapers
(sunset, ocean, terraces, dusk and lagoon) each have one strong hue, which the Dock tint takes on; the others are the
thumbnails of the videos in Finder.
